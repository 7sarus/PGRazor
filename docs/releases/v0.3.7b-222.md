# PGRazor v0.3.7b-222

## New features

- **Joystick lock:** Hold the joystick outward to pop out a lock badge on its edge. Drag onto the badge to lock the joystick at that direction, and tap to unlock.

## Improvements & fixes

- Locked joystick keeps steering in its direction until you unlock it.
